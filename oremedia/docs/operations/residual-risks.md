# Residual risks before the client pilot (ledger 7.g2)

Owners are roles until the pilot names people (Engineering lead, Platform engineer, Security lead, Product owner,
Tenant success). Exposure = who or what is hit if it happens; likelihood is a judgement for the pilot scale (two
client brands, a handful of channels); control = what exists today and what closes it.
