# Caption checklist

Before returning a variant, confirm each line:

1. Opens with the audience's situation or question, not with the brand name.
2. Every claim maps to an approved fact id; the fact's wording is preserved in meaning.
3. No avoided term, no prohibited phrase, no restricted topic.
4. Locale, spelling and currency follow the brand's default locale unless the brief says otherwise.
5. Length and hashtag count follow the channel guidance (when none is given: under 2,200 characters, at most five
   hashtags, no hashtags for LinkedIn beyond three).
6. One CTA, phrased the way the channel guidance describes.
7. The rationale names the angle, the insight used and the fact ids.
